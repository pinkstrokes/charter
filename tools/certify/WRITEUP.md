## One line
Charter knows every situation its skills can face, proved, and earns autonomy situation by situation; it found a way to chase a customer with its own money that six months of history never produced.

## Paragraph
**Every situation, before it happens.** A replay shows how a skill did on the payments it has seen. Charter's competence map also asks what it will do in situations it has not seen.
- A solver enumerates every situation the cash skill's versions can tell apart: 40, none missing, under consistency axioms every recorded payment satisfies.
- Each is realized as a concrete payment, a few edits of a real ledger, read by the seat's own code.
- Company B's current version would act alone in 16 situations that none of its 1,225 payments ever fell into. B's and C's current versions disagree in 5 that neither company has seen; in one of them the reference and the remittance name different invoices.
- Earning autonomy situation by situation instead of rule by rule, replayed on every decision without hindsight, halves B's mistakes made alone (14 to 7) for 21 more questions in six months. Each of those questions is the first in its situation, so it can be asked before a payment waits.
- Checked across seats and the kernel, the map found a team failure: a franchisee pays everything it owes with a vague reference, cash application rightly asks a person, and collections sends a past-due notice and $300 of late fees the same day. No seat is wrong on its own, and six months never produced it.
- The obvious policy stops the notice but not the fees, because a fee reaches the kernel without facts. The solver proves which fixes hold.

## Track notes
- **Spacetime:** the map's checks run against the same TypeScript the module executes (dsl.ts, matching.ts, policy.ts, labels.ts); nothing is reimplemented for the proof.
- **Microsoft, "Built for the Real World":** a path to production for learned automation in finance. Certify every version against the situations it has not seen, and fence the unverified ones to a person.
