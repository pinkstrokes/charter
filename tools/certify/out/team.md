# Team invariant: never chase a counterparty with its own money

Checked across the collections skill (each of company A's versions, as dsl.ts runs it), the kernel (policy.ts evaluate over all 512 combinations of its 9 facts) and the grace guard (labels.ts), for every possible situation; the axioms on the collections features hold on all 946 of company A's collections decisions. Four properties: no notice while their cash covers what is past due; no late fee while their cash covers what is past due; no late fee in bankruptcy; never holds a chase their cash does not cover.

On the runs' own books before the fix, notices went out while the counterparty's unapplied cash covered what was past due: company B 72 of 1024 notices, to 7 customers; C 34 of 1086, to 8; C from scratch 45 of 1110; A 0 of 5.
